# Purchasing Policy

**Status:** DRAFT R0, October 1, 2026. Not yet adopted. Owner: Max Wamsley, CEO. Effective date: [INSERT EFFECTIVE DATE.]

**Scope:** purchases for Clarus Labs that are not travel. Travel follows the travel policy and is submitted in the Travel app.

## 1. The short version

Clarus buys what the work needs, at a fair price, with approval before the money is spent and a receipt afterward. Most purchases are made by the approver. You ask in the Purchase Requests app, the approver approves and buys, and the receipt goes on the request. If you buy something yourself, the rules in Section 4 apply.

This policy applies to employees and to anyone else who buys on behalf of Clarus. The numbers below match what the app enforces.

## 2. How a purchase is made

1. Open the Purchase Requests app and start a request. Give one business purpose and, if a grant pays for it, the project or grant code.
2. Add one row for each item: the vendor, what it is and why we need it, the estimated amount, the category, and the web address of the item. If there is no web page, say why.
3. Attach a quote when a vendor total is $500 or more (Section 5).
4. Tick the certification and send the request to the approver. The approver is the site Owners, today Max Wamsley.
5. The approver approves, buys, changes each row to what was actually paid, attaches the receipt or invoice, and marks the request purchased. You have nothing more to do unless the request is returned with a note.

Every request that the approver buys goes to the approver, whatever the amount.

## 3. Approval

| Who buys | When approval is needed |
|---|---|
| The approver | Every request, whatever the amount. Approval and purchase are one step. |
| The employee | A vendor total of $500 or more within one request, before the purchase. Under $500 needs no approval, but the request is still submitted with receipts. |

- **Thresholds count by vendor, not by row.** Several rows from the same vendor in one request add up. Splitting a purchase into pieces to stay under $500 is not allowed.
- **Who approves.** The site Owners approve. An Owner may approve their own request. It is recorded as self-approved, and the record, the email and the CSV say so.
- **Changes after approval (employee-bought requests).** If a vendor total ends up more than 10% above the approved amount, or a new vendor total reaches $500, the request goes back for approval before it is submitted. Spending less never needs new approval. When the approver buys, the approver is the one spending, so this check does not apply.
- **A purchase already made.** If an employee buys something over $500 before approval, the request can still be sent, and it is flagged "Bought before approval". The approver decides whether to approve it. This is an exception, not a way to work.

## 4. When an employee buys it

The company card or an invoice to Clarus is the default. An employee may buy something and be reimbursed when that is the practical way to get it.

- Approval follows Section 3: none under $500 per vendor, approval first at $500 or more.
- Every row needs a receipt or invoice, or a written reason there is none.
- Submit the request within 30 days of the purchase date. Reimbursement covers the rows marked as paid by the employee, and only for purchases made under this policy.

## 5. Quotes

A vendor total of $500 or more needs a quote attached to the request, or a written reason there is none, such as "Already purchased" or a sole source. The approver reads the quote or the reason as part of the approval.

## 6. Receipts, certification and records

- **Receipts.** A receipt or invoice is required for every purchase. One receipt may cover several rows. If there is truly none, the request says why in writing.
- **Certification.** The employee who sends the request ticks this, in these exact words: "I certify that the listed purchases are for official Clarus Labs business purposes, are not personal expenses, have not been reimbursed elsewhere, and that the information provided is accurate to the best of my knowledge." When the employee buys it, the tick is at submission instead.
- **Records.** When a request is purchased or submitted, the app builds a folder with the receipts, the quotes and a CSV file, and emails the administrator. The administrator processes it in QuickBooks and files it. Records are kept for [INSERT RECORD RETENTION PERIOD.]

## 8. Purchases paid with grant funds

- **Every grant purchase carries the project or grant code** on the request.
- **The approver checks that the purchase is allowable under the award** before approving. If the award terms and this policy differ, the award terms rule.
- **Equipment over $5,000 per item needs prior written approval from the NSF program officer.** The approver does not approve or buy the item until that approval is in hand. Keep it with the request. The $5,000 applies to each item, not to the order total.

This section covers only grant funds. It does not change how equipment is booked in QuickBooks (Section 7).

## 9. What Clarus does not buy

- Personal items or personal expenses.
- Anything that has been, or will be, reimbursed elsewhere.
- Gifts and entertainment, unless the CEO approves them in writing first.
- Splitting a purchase to avoid a threshold (Section 3).

## 10. Who does what

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Employee | Requests only what the work needs, gives accurate details, attaches quotes where required, and certifies the request. |
| Approver | Approves before the money is spent, confirms categories, checks grant allowability, and when buying, records what was actually paid and attaches the receipt. |
| Administrator | Processes the request in QuickBooks, decides the account for Equipment and Other, files the folder, and marks the request processed. |

## 11. Exceptions and changes

The CEO may approve an exception to this policy, in writing, before the purchase. Only the CEO changes this policy, in writing, and the app, the SOP and this policy are updated together so the numbers always match.
