## 2. How the quiz works (for Max)

- 15 questions, one point each. You need 14 or more to pass.
- Forms scores each attempt and keeps the name, the score and the time. Forms cannot stop anyone from skipping it or tell you who passed by itself, so you look at the Responses tab.
- Retakes are allowed. Forms shows the correct answers after each attempt, so the quiz teaches as well as checks.
- The questions cover only what the app and the SOP do today. The 30-day deadline and the $5,000 grant equipment approval are in the policy draft but are not adopted or built. Add those questions when the policy is adopted.

## 3. The quiz

Source for every question: `docs/SOP.md`, Part A (the same text as the Instructions panel in the app). The "SOP section" line names the heading.

### Q1
Question: You need to book a flight to a conference and also buy a $40 box of pipette tips. Where does each one go?
A. Both go in the Purchase Requests app, because the conference is for work.
B. The flight goes in the Travel app. The pipette tips go in the Purchase Requests app.
C. The flight goes in the Purchase Requests app. The pipette tips go in the Travel app.
D. Post both in the Purchasing Receipt Team on Teams, as before.
Correct: B
Feedback: Not quite. Travel costs go in the Travel app, other purchases go here, and posting in Teams is retired. See SOP: What goes in a purchase request.
SOP section: What goes in a purchase request

### Q2
Question: You start a request for a $60 pack of lab filters and leave the choice of who buys it as it is. What happens after you send it?
A. It is under $500, so nobody needs to approve it, and you buy the filters and submit your own receipt.
B. You buy the filters with your own card, and the company reimburses you once the receipt is in.
C. The request goes to the approver, who approves it, buys the filters, attaches the receipt and marks it purchased.
D. The administrator buys the filters once the request has been checked and processed in QuickBooks.
Correct: C
Feedback: Not quite. The approver buys by default, and every such request goes to the approver whatever the amount. See SOP: When the approver buys it.
SOP section: Starting a request; When the approver buys it

### Q3
Question: The approver will buy the item. What do you enter on each row?
A. The vendor, what it is and why, an estimated amount, the category, and the item's web address or why there is none.
B. The vendor, what it is and why, the receipt, who paid, and the date it was bought.
C. The vendor, the exact date it was bought, the final amount, and the approver's name.
D. The vendor, what it is and why, the category, and a quote attached to every row.
Correct: A
Feedback: Not quite. You give the vendor, what and why, an estimated amount, the category and the item link. You attach no receipt, and nobody asks who paid. See SOP: When the approver buys it.
SOP section: When the approver buys it

### Q4
Question: The approver will buy it. The item you need is a custom part from a local machine shop, and it has no web page. What do you do on that row?
A. Leave the Item link empty and send the request. The approver will find the item.
B. Type "none" in the Item link box so that the row is not empty.
C. Ask the approver to type a link in later, after they approve the request.
D. Say why there is no web page in the "No web page: say why" box that appears.
Correct: D
Feedback: Not quite. Every row needs the item link or a reason in the "No web page: say why" box. See SOP: When the approver buys it.
SOP section: When the approver buys it

### Q5
Question: You ask the approver to buy a $620 centrifuge rotor from one vendor, on one row. What does the request need?
A. Nothing extra, because the approver is buying it and will keep the receipt.
B. A quote attached, or a reason there is none in the "No quote: say why" box.
C. A receipt attached to the row, because the amount is over $500.
D. A second approver's signature, because the amount is over $500.
Correct: B
Feedback: Not quite. A vendor total of $500 or more needs a quote or a reason there is none, whoever buys it. The receipt comes later from whoever buys. See SOP: When the approver buys it.
SOP section: When the approver buys it

### Q6
Question: You choose "I will buy it myself". You need $800 of items from one vendor, and you enter them as two rows of $400 so that each row is under $500. What is true?
A. It is fine. Each row is under $500, so no approval is needed before you buy.
B. The app rejects the second row because it has the same vendor as the first.
C. The vendor total is $800, so you need the approver's approval before you buy. Splitting does not avoid that.
D. You only need approval if the two rows have the same date, because then they count as one purchase.
Correct: C
Feedback: Not quite. Approval is worked out by vendor within one request, so splitting rows does not avoid it. See SOP: When you buy it yourself, approval and quotes.
SOP section: When you buy it yourself: approval and quotes

### Q7
Question: You choose "I will buy it myself". One request has $300 of items from Vendor A and $300 of items from Vendor B. Do you need approval before you buy?
A. No approval is needed, because neither vendor's total reaches $500. You still submit with receipts.
B. Yes. The request total is $600, which is over $500, so you need approval first.
C. Yes. A request with more than one vendor always needs approval before you buy.
D. No approval and no receipts are needed, because every row is under $500.
Correct: A
Feedback: Not quite. Approval is worked out by vendor. If every vendor total is under $500, no approval is needed, but you still submit the request with receipts. See SOP: When you buy it yourself, approval and quotes.
SOP section: When you buy it yourself: approval and quotes

### Q8
Question: The approver approved $1,000 from one vendor, and you bought it yourself. After a price increase, the vendor total comes to $1,150. What do you do?
A. Nothing. The approval still covers the purchase, so you submit as usual.
B. Delete $150 from the request so that it matches the approved amount.
C. Submit it as it is. The approver will see the higher amount when it arrives.
D. Send the request for approval again. The total is more than 10% above the approved amount.
Correct: D
Feedback: Not quite. A vendor total that rises more than 10% above the approved amount needs approval again. A small rise, such as tax or shipping, and any lower amount do not. See SOP: When you buy it yourself, approval and quotes.
SOP section: When you buy it yourself: approval and quotes

### Q9
Question: You bought something yourself for $700 before asking for approval, and you have the receipt. What should you do?
A. Nothing. A purchase made before approval cannot be recorded in the app.
B. Send the request for approval anyway. It is flagged "Bought before approval", and the approver decides.
C. Change the purchase date to a later day so that the request is not flagged.
D. Mark the row as paid by the company, so that no approval is needed.
Correct: B
Feedback: Not quite. You can still send it for approval. It is flagged "Bought before approval", and the approver decides. Ask for approval before you buy whenever you can. See SOP: Bought something before approval?
SOP section: Bought something before approval?

### Q10
Question: Which statement about categories is correct?
A. You suggest a category for each row, and the approver or administrator can confirm or change it.
B. You choose one category for the whole request, and it cannot be changed after you send it.
C. Choose Other whenever you are unsure. You do not need to describe it.
D. You type the QuickBooks account number for each row yourself.
Correct: A
Feedback: Not quite. Each category is a QuickBooks account. You suggest one for each row, and the approver or administrator confirms or changes it. Choose Other only when nothing fits, and describe it. See SOP: Categories.
SOP section: Categories

### Q11
Question: You need an annual license for an analysis program that renews every year. Which category fits best?
A. Computer, H/W & S/W Supplies
B. Office Supplies
C. Dues and Subscriptions
D. Equipment
Correct: C
Feedback: Not quite. Recurring software goes under Dues and Subscriptions. One-time computer hardware and software goes under Computer, H/W & S/W Supplies. See SOP: Categories.
SOP section: Categories

### Q12
Question: You choose "I will buy it myself" and pay for an item with your personal credit card. What do you choose for "Who paid"?
A. Company. It is a Clarus purchase for Clarus business.
B. Leave it blank. The approver fills in who paid.
C. Either one. Both are reimbursed in the same way.
D. Employee. You paid, so Clarus reimburses you.
Correct: D
Feedback: Not quite. Employee means you paid and Clarus reimburses you. Company means Clarus paid, by company card or invoice. You are asked only when you buy it yourself. See SOP: Who paid.
SOP section: Who paid

### Q13
Question: You bought it yourself. One row has only a quote attached and no receipt. What do you need before you can submit?
A. Nothing. A quote counts as a receipt for the row.
B. The approver's signature on the quote, to show it was accepted.
C. A receipt or invoice for the row, or a reason there is none in the "No receipt: say why" box.
D. A photo of your card statement showing the charge.
Correct: C
Feedback: Not quite. When you buy it yourself, every row needs a receipt or invoice, or a reason there is none. A quote is not a receipt. See SOP: No receipt?
SOP section: No receipt?

### Q14
Question: The approver is buying your request. When do you tick the certification, and what does it say?
A. When the approver marks it purchased. It says the items arrived, match what you asked for, and were paid for by Clarus.
B. When you send the request to the approver. It says the purchases are for official Clarus business, not personal, not reimbursed elsewhere, and accurate.
C. When the approver approves it, and the approver ticks it for you. It says the purchase is allowed under Clarus policy and the grant.
D. Only when the request is $500 or more, after you buy. It says that you received a quote and compared prices.
Correct: B
Feedback: Not quite. When the approver buys it, you tick the certification when you send the request. It covers official business purposes, no personal expenses, no reimbursement elsewhere, and accuracy. See SOP: Certification.
SOP section: Certification

### Q15
Question: The approver returns your request with a note. What do you do?
A. Start a new request and ignore the old one, because a returned request cannot be edited.
B. Email the approver your corrections and wait for them to update the request.
C. Nothing. A returned request is cancelled and the approver buys nothing.
D. Open it, correct what the note asks for, and send it to the approver again.
Correct: D
Feedback: Not quite. A returned request shows the approver's note. Correct it and send it again. See SOP: When the approver buys it.
SOP section: When the approver buys it
